01Project overview

Insul Techs is a family-owned insulation company founded by Luis Ramos in 2016. It works out of Martinez and Concord and offers spray foam, injection foam, K-13 coatings, fireproofing and firestop systems, plus the Fireplace Techs and demolition divisions.

Most work today comes from referrals and from Genesis's relationships with contractors. The goal is steady growth and more commercial and higher-value work: hotels, builders and construction companies, and premium markets like Mill Valley and Napa.

What this sprint is for

  • Fix the foundation so Google trusts the site and the Google Business Profile
  • Establish a San Jose presence (Maria's plan) so contractors and builders in the South Bay find you
  • Point the site at searches that turn into jobs, not definitions
  • Make sure every lead from the site reaches your CRM and your team
  • Leave you with a clear 90-day report and a recommended next step

03Scope of work

Foundation first, then build. There's no point adding pages to a site that can't hold them. Your team keeps email, text and social. This covers the website, SEO and Google Business Profile side.

Phase 1 · Foundation

Cleanup + San Jose setup

  • Onboarding call, access handover and baseline report
  • Title tags and meta descriptions rewritten on the homepage and key service pages
  • Demo copy removed; duplicate blocks and heading structure fixed
  • One official name, address and phone across the site, Google, Yelp, BBB and Diamond Certified
  • Internal training pages pulled from Google
  • Google Business Profile cleanup: categories, services, photos
  • San Jose Google Business Profile set up once the San Jose address is confirmed
  • Search Console + Analytics checked and connected
Month 2 · Build

Pages that bring in calls

  • San Jose location page
  • Top service pages rewritten for "hire me" searches: spray foam, attic insulation, insulation removal
  • A commercial page aimed at builders, GCs and property managers
  • Lead path tested end to end: website form → your CRM → your team
  • Google Business Profile posts started
  • Monthly report
Month 3 · Momentum

Expand + prove it

  • Additional city pages (East Bay, South Bay; Marin/Napa as premium targets)
  • Internal linking so pages support each other
  • Review request flow set up in your CRM after each completed job
  • Plan for moving Fireplaces + Demolition onto the main domain
  • 90-day report with before/after numbers and the recommended next step

The Spanish version runs on a translation plugin, so English updates carry over automatically. Where it needs a human, I've got it.

08Terms

  1. Commitment. This is a three-month engagement (Phase 1 plus months 2 and 3). After that it continues month to month until either side gives 30 days' written notice.
  2. Payment. The deposit is non-refundable once work begins. Monthly payments are due on the 1st of each month. Work pauses if a payment is more than 10 days late.
  3. Ownership. Everything built for Insul Techs belongs to Insul Techs: pages, content, and every account (website, domain, Google Business Profile, Search Console, Analytics). Kaizen Agency works as a collaborator on your accounts and hands back any access when the engagement ends.
  4. Results. SEO depends on Google, competitors and timing, so no specific ranking or lead count is guaranteed. What's guaranteed is the work in section 03, done well and on schedule, with a report every month.
  5. Approvals. Nothing goes live on your site or profiles without Genesis's or Luis's approval. Delays in access or approvals shift the timeline, not the price.
  6. Confidentiality. Your business information, CRM data and customer details stay private and are used only for this work.
