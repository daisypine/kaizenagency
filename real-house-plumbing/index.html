Why the site isn't ranking

The site looks like every other plumber, and Google can tell.

The site was built quickly with an AI website builder. That's a fine start, but Google rewards real, specific information about a real business, and right now there's very little of it.

1

Leftover template text

When the site is shared on Facebook, text or Google, the preview title reads "Your Plumbing Company | Palo Alto, CA", the builder's placeholder, not your name.

2

AI-generated photos, no real ones

The photos on the homepage are ChatGPT-generated images, and the same image file is used twice. No photos of your team, trucks or jobs.

3

27 copy-paste city pages

One page per city across San Mateo, Santa Clara and Alameda counties, all saying roughly the same thing. Google treats that as thin content, which is why it ranks you around Berkeley and Dublin instead of around Palo Alto.

4

Details that don't match

The homepage says 15+ years; the site's share description says 20 years. The $200-off water heater coupon uses the code HEAT100. Two sections ("Why Homeowners Call Us First", "Trusted By Local Businesses") have headings but no content.
