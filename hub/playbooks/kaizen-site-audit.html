Kaizen Site Audit (existing / inherited sites)

For new builds and launches use kaizen-build-base (its security and post-launch audits still apply). This SOP is for sites Kaizen takes over or revisits, first run on Falcon Roofing (Oct 2026).

Output

A "Cleanup Log" tab in the client's sitemap sheet: Priority (Critical/High/Medium/Done) · Item · Where · Status · Owner · Needs OK from · Notes. Most urgent first, Done items at the bottom with dates.

1. Admin checks (wp-admin)

  • Plugins: list active/inactive and pending updates. Flag banned plugins (see kaizen-build-base), duplicates doing the same job (two header/footer snippet plugins, two form plugins, two consent plugins), heavy plugins (Broken Link Checker), unused plugins.

  • Themes: active theme + parent; unused default themes to delete.

  • Users: admin count matches the list, no unknown admins, correct emails. Blog author is the owner, not "office".

  • Code snippets (WPCode, HFCM): what each does, inactive or dangerous ones (search-replace on init).

  • Settings: "Discourage search engines" off, timezone correct, permalinks.

  • Consent plugin: Complianz is standard (see kaizen-legal-pages).

2. Front-end checks (logged out)

Verify with fetch(url + '?v=' + Date.now(), {credentials:'omit'}) or a private window, after purging SG cache.

  • Header/footer: phone numbers and offices current, license number (contractors, B&P 7030.5), legal links.

  • Dead links (#/ placeholders, links to deleted pages), "View all" links.

  • Duplicate pages (hubs copied from another page, two pages targeting one keyword).

  • Typos, wrong years, unsourced stats and superlatives, fake or repeated testimonials.

  • Heading order (one H1, no H2 label above it), alt text.

  • Forms: fields, steps, where submissions go; notice at collection.

  • Third-party scripts and iframes (chat widgets, pixels, maps) - remove unknown ones or disclose them.

  • Schema: check JSON-LD on home and key pages (parse pretty-printed JSON, don't regex). LocalBusiness/RoofingContractor on home + location pages.

3. SEO data

  • GSC: indexed vs not indexed with reasons (noindex, redirect, canonical, 404). 301 real 404s. Queries with high impressions and low CTR → rewrite titles/meta.

  • GA4: key events set.

  • GBP: verified profiles, duplicates to merge, NAP matches the site.

  • Competitor gap: crawl the client's sitemap and the named competitor's, compare page by page into a Competitor Gap tab.

4. Fix vs ask

Fix directly (then verify logged out): typos, dead links, wrong numbers, alt text, titles/meta, schema additions, timezone. Needs Daisy's OK first: deleting pages/plugins/themes/snippets, publishing new pages (stay Drafts), permission/user changes, removing offices, anything client-facing that states addresses, customer names or prices. Needs the client: credentials, warranties, addresses, legal name.

5. Editing tips (Elementor)

Wait for elementor.getPreviewContainer(), walk containers recursively, change with $e.run('document/elements/settings'), save with $e.run('document/save/publish'), confirm elementor.saver.isEditorChanged() is false. Strip ?=&;% from JS output in Chrome.

6. Wrap-up

Update the Cleanup Log and the sitemap Overview counts, move board cards, and send Daisy a short summary: what was fixed, what needs her OK, what needs the client.

Public page. No passwords, keys, client contact details or prices here; those live in Drive and the workspace.
