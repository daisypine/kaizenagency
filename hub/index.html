Who does what

  • Daisy Strategy, pricing and money, client calls, final approval on anything that goes live or goes to a client.
  • Basil Runs the projects day to day: the board, deadlines, asset chasing, client updates, QA. Hands work to the designer and developer, or figures it out.
  • Claude + a person Claude drafts, researches and checks (briefs, sitemaps, copy, audits, posts, reports). A person reviews before anything goes out.

When Daisy's out

  1. You decide everything except strategy, money, or unusual technical risk. Those wait for Daisy.
  2. Nothing goes live and nothing goes to a client without Daisy's OK in writing. If it's urgent and she's unreachable, hold it and note it on the card.
  3. Every project always has a next action and a due date on Command Center. If it doesn't, it isn't being managed.
  4. When you answer a question that will come up again, add it to the checklist or a playbook so nobody has to ask twice.

Your daily rhythm

Morning

Review every Command Center card. Flag overdue or blocked. Pick your top 3. Update cards before working. Post one summary to Daisy: done / next / blocked / decisions. (There's a prompt for this.)

Afternoon

Update status. Attach proof (screenshot, link). Re-date anything unfinished. Tell owners about blockers.

Limits

Max 3 active projects at once: one finish-and-launch, one next cash project, one recurring client deliverable.

Weekly

Monday 30-min board review and Friday 20-min review with Daisy.

Using Claude

  1. Open Claude and start a new chat. For a client you'll work on a lot, make a Claude Project and add their brief, transcript and hub link.
  2. Copy a prompt from the prompt library, fill in the brackets.
  3. Prompts that start with "Read ..." point Claude at a playbook page here, so it follows the same steps Daisy's skills do.
  4. Claude can't log in to WordPress, the workspace or client accounts for you. You click, it tells you what to click and checks what you see.
  5. Check every fact before it goes anywhere. Claude is fast, not always right.

Rules that never bend

  • Real facts only. No invented numbers, licenses, reviews, quotes or projects.
  • Never share passwords in chat, Slack, email or Claude. One account per person, with 2FA.
  • No private client data on GitHub. These pages are public.
  • Client copy: short hyphens only, no long dashes, no homeowner names.
  • Keep sites clean and fast. No plugins outside the build base stack.
