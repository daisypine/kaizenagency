When Daisy's out

  1. You decide everything except strategy, money, or unusual technical risk. Those wait for Daisy.
  2. Nothing goes live and nothing goes to a client without Daisy's OK in writing. If it's urgent and she can't be reached, hold it and note it on the card.
  3. Every project always has a next action and a due date on Command Center.
  4. Answered a question that will come up again? Add it to the process or a playbook.

Who does what

Daisy Strategy, pricing and money, client calls, final approval on anything that goes live or goes to a client.

Basil Runs projects day to day: the board, deadlines, chasing assets, client updates, QA. Hands work to the designer and developer, or figures it out.

Claude + a person Claude drafts, researches and checks. A person reviews before anything goes out.

Your day

Morning. Review every Command Center card, flag overdue or blocked, pick your top 3, update cards before working, post one summary to Daisy (prompt).

Afternoon. Update status, attach proof, re-date anything unfinished, tell owners about blockers.

Limit. 3 active projects: one finish-and-launch, one next cash project, one recurring client deliverable.

Weekly. Monday 30-minute and Friday 20-minute board reviews with Daisy.

Using Claude

  1. Start a chat. For a client you'll work on a lot, make a Claude Project with their brief, transcript and hub link.
  2. Copy a prompt from the prompt library and fill in the brackets.
  3. Prompts that start with "Read ..." point Claude at a playbook here, so it follows the same steps as Daisy's skills.
  4. Claude can't log in for you. You click; it tells you what to click and checks what you see.
  5. Check every fact before it goes anywhere.
