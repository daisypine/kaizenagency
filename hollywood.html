The one that matters most

The $5 Universal parking is the biggest single win and it's the easiest to blow. Buy the movie ticket the night before, screenshot the barcode, and have it open at the tollbooth. If you show up without it you pay full price and then have to stand in the AMC box office line for a cash rebate.

Also worth knowing Beverly Hills city garages give the first 2 hours free — good for a Rodeo Drive photo stop instead of feeding a meter.

Day 1 — Getting there Tue · Sep 22

Two routes, roughly six hours each, landing within minutes of each other. The time zone does you a favor.

7:30 AM
PT

Leave Gilroy Daisy

≈330 mi · 5h10 free-flow · plan 6h15 door to door

Route US-101 S the entire way — Salinas, Paso Robles, SLO, Santa Barbara, Ventura, then into the Valley and down through Hollywood.

Traffic Clean until Ventura. The pinch is 101 through Woodland Hills / Sherman Oaks, which starts stacking up around 2:30 PM. Leaving at 7:30 puts you ahead of it. Leaving at 9 does not.

Alt CA-152 → I-5 S over the Grapevine is 20–30 min faster on a good day and miserable on a bad one. 101 is the safer call for a day you can't be late.

1:45 PM

Meet — Hollywood Hotel Both

1160 N Vermont Ave, Los Angeles, CA 90029 · (323) 746-0444

Heads up on the address The Google result you screenshotted was for "The Hotel Hollywood" on Yucca St — a completely different property. Yours is on Vermont, in East Hollywood near Los Feliz.

Check-in is 3:00 but the front desk will hold bags, and people do get let in early when rooms are ready. Ask. Worst case you sit by the pool for an hour.

Your room has a fridge — that's what makes the burrito plan work later.

15 min

Santa Monica Pier

Parking The deck right on the pier is the most expensive option. The downtown structures on 2nd and 4th are cheaper and it's a 5-minute walk. ≈$15

One hour is enough — walk out to the end, look back at the coastline, skip the arcade unless you want it.

7:00 PM

Venice Beach — sunset at 7:20

Ocean Front Walk lot, 3099 Ocean Front Walk, Venice, CA 90291 · $6 15 min down Ocean Ave

This is the right place to catch the sunset — over the water, not over Hollywood. Walk the boardwalk north toward the skate park while the light is good.

Timing + safety The boardwalk empties out fast once it's fully dark. Be back at the car by 8:15, and don't leave anything visible in either car — this is the one lot on the whole trip where that matters most.

8:15 PM

Decision point

Option A — your original plan. Drive back for the Walk of Fame lit up. I-10 E → La Brea north, ≈50 min in evening traffic, there by 9:10. Park in the Ovation Hollywood garage at Hollywood & Highland. Back to the hotel around 10:30, which gives you about 7 hours before a 5:45 wake-up.

Option B — what I'd do. Skip it tonight and drive straight back (≈40 min, in bed by 10). Your Thursday hotel is three blocks from the Walk of Fame — you can do it on foot, for free, with a whole free afternoon, instead of tacking an hour of night driving onto a day that started with a six-hour drive.

Same sights, better day, more sleep before the one morning that actually has a hard start time.

Day 2 — Universal Wed · Sep 23

Park 9 AM–6 PM, early access at 8. This is the day with a real clock on it, and the day the two-hotel logistics get solved.

Before you sleep Tuesday night

  • Buy the 7 PM AMC movie ticket in the app. Screenshot the parking barcode. This is your $5 parking.
  • Confirm the Super Nintendo World Early Access add-on. It's a separate paid ticket, roughly $20–30 per person, and it sells out in advance — it is not included with admission. If you haven't bought it, buy it tonight or the 8 AM plan doesn't exist.
  • Call the Franklin and confirm they'll hold luggage before check-in. Front desk is 24 hours.
  • Pack everything except what you're wearing. Nothing gets packed at 6 AM.

Load both cars. Coffee wherever is open on Vermont, or hold out for CityWalk.

Park, then walk

The walk from the structure to the front gate is longer than anyone expects — escalators, CityWalk, then the gate. Give it a full 15 minutes. Gates typically open around 7:30 for early access holders.

8:00 AM

Super Nintendo World — early access hour

Mario Kart: Bowser's Challenge first, immediately. Later in the day this line hits 90+ minutes. Then Yoshi's Adventure, then the key challenges around the land.

The part people miss Your Early Access ticket also includes one Express-lane ride on the Studio Tour, valid until 11 AM. Keep the ticket. Use it around 10:15 when the standby line is already 45–60 min.

9:00 AM

Lower Lot, before it fills

Everyone else is sprinting to Nintendo right now, which you've already done. Go down to Jurassic World: The Ride and Revenge of the Mummy while the Lower Lot is still quiet. You'll get soaked on Jurassic — worth planning your clothes around.

10:15 AM

Studio Tour — Express lane

About an hour. It's the thing Universal Hollywood has that no other park does, so don't treat it as filler.

11:30 AM

Brunch at the diner attached to the hotel

There's an adjoining door from the Franklin straight into the diner next door. On a slow morning that's the whole appeal — you don't move a car.

12:15 PM

Erewhon — the $100 mission

Erewhon Grove · 7660 Beverly Blvd, Los Angeles, CA 90036 · 15 min Closer alt: 4121 Santa Monica Blvd, 90029 · 12 min, has complimentary valet

The Grove location is the bigger flagship if you want the full experience and the smoothies. The Santa Monica Blvd one is closer and easier to park at.

Spend it well Don't just buy snacks — build a picnic for the Bowl. The Hollywood Bowl lets you bring your own food in for most shows, so a $100 Erewhon haul becomes dinner, concert snacks, and Friday's road food instead of just a shopping bag. Verify the food/alcohol policy on this specific event page, since promoter-rented shows sometimes restrict it.

2:00 PM

Somewhere you haven't been

If you want more Mexican food and something new at once: Grand Central Market downtown, or Olvera Street next to Union Station. Both are ~20 min and both are a different LA than the one you've seen so far this trip.

4:00 PM

Walk of Fame — on foot, finally

5 min walk from the hotel

Down Highland to Hollywood Blvd. TCL Chinese Theatre handprints, Ovation Hollywood, Dolby Theatre, the stars themselves. No parking, no meter, no circling. This is the payoff for skipping it Tuesday night.

5:30 PM

Back to the room

Shower, change, pack the picnic. It gets cool at the Bowl after dark even in September — bring a layer.

6:30 PM

Walk to the Hollywood Bowl

2301 N Highland Ave, Los Angeles, CA 90068 0.8 mi · ≈18 min · uphill at the end

Doors opened at 6:00. Getting there early is the move — people eat at the picnic tables before the show and it's half the experience.

On the parking you already bought Bowl parking is mostly stacked, meaning you're blocked in until the cars around you leave. Walking in and out is faster than driving 0.8 miles, and you skip the post-show gridlock entirely. Treat the parking spend as sunk unless your lot happens to be somewhere convenient.

Small things that make this work

  • A soft cooler bag + a couple of ice packs. Makes the extra burrito viable, keeps the Erewhon haul alive, and matters on a 6-hour drive each way.
  • SpotHero installed before you leave, for Rodni's two-day garage spot.
  • Screenshot everything — hotel confirmations, Universal tickets, the AMC parking barcode, Bowl tickets. Cell service at the Bowl gets rough when 17,000 people arrive at once.
  • Nothing visible in either car, ever. Especially the Venice lot Tuesday and the street spot Wednesday–Friday.
  • A layer for Thursday night. The Bowl is outdoors, in a canyon, after dark.
  • Comfortable shoes for Wednesday. Universal is roughly 6–8 miles of walking with a serious hill between the Upper and Lower Lots.
