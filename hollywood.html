Day 1 — Getting there Tue · Sep 22

Two routes, landing about half an hour apart. Check-in isn't until 3, so the afternoon is built around that.

7:15 AM
PT

Leave Gilroy Daisy

≈320 mi · 5h drive + ~1h charging & stops · plan 6h

Route CA-152 E over Pacheco Pass → I-5 S → Grapevine → I-5 S → CA-170 S → US-101 S into Hollywood.

Charging Leave full. Kettleman City is the natural stop — roughly 155 mi in, the biggest Supercharger on the route, real restrooms and food. One 25–30 min stop there should do it; Tejon Ranch (just before the Grapevine, ~250 mi in) is your backup if you'd rather split it or want a buffer for LA driving. Los Banos and Buttonwillow are also on the line if you need them.

Traffic I-5 is clean midday. The two things to watch are truck congestion on the Grapevine descent into Santa Clarita, and the 170 → 101 handoff, which starts stacking around 2:30 PM. Arriving by 1:15 puts you well ahead of it.

8:00 AM
MT

Leave St George Rodni

≈385 mi · 5h50 free-flow · plan 6h45 door to door

Time zone 8:00 AM in St George is 7:00 AM in LA. You get the hour back on arrival — 6h45 of driving lands at 1:45 PM Pacific.

Route I-15 S through Mesquite, Vegas, Primm, Baker, Barstow → Cajon Pass → I-215 S → CA-210 W → CA-134 W → US-101 S.

Gas — keep it cheap Leave St George full. Top off in Mesquite or Las Vegas, which are the cheapest fuel on the whole route. Skip Primm and Baker entirely — they're captive-market stops and the most expensive gas on the I-15 corridor, often a dollar-plus over Vegas. If you need one more, Barstow is reasonable. Get into LA with enough range that you never have to buy fuel in the city, and fill in Barstow again on Friday's way home rather than in Hollywood.

7:00 PM

Venice Beach — picnic & sunset

Ocean Front Walk lot, 3099 Ocean Front Walk, Venice 90291 · $6 15 min

Sunset at 7:20. Eat on the sand facing west. This is the right call over Santa Monica — the pier is prettier from a distance than it is up close, and Venice puts the sun straight over the water in front of you.

After dark The boardwalk empties out quickly once the light goes. Plan to be back at the car by around 8:15, and leave nothing visible inside — this is the one lot all trip where that matters most.

8:15 PM

Open block — pick one or two, then home

On the way back, no detour: the burritos at 3118 S Sepulveda Blvd (15 min, straight up Lincoln → I-10 E) or the ramen at 2123 Sawtelle Blvd (15 min). Grab the extra burrito here for tomorrow — it goes in the hotel fridge overnight, and honestly the best version of that plan is eating it in the car at 7 AM rather than trying to keep it cold until noon inside the park.

If you want one more stop: the Tesla Diner at 7001 Santa Monica Blvd is open 24 hours, has Superchargers out front, and is 10 min from the hotel. Burgers plus a top-off before Universal day, which is genuinely useful given tomorrow starts at 5:45.

Skip tonight: Santa Monica and the Walk of Fame. Santa Monica pulls you the wrong direction, and the Walk of Fame is three blocks from Thursday's hotel — you can do it in daylight Thursday afternoon or at night after the concert, for free, on foot.

Target: back at the hotel and winding down by 10:30. Tomorrow is the one day with a hard clock on it.

Small things that make this work

  • A soft cooler bag + ice packs. Makes the extra burrito viable, keeps the Erewhon haul alive, and matters on two six-hour drives.
  • A blanket or two towels for the Venice picnic.
  • SpotHero booked before Wednesday, for Rodni's two-day spot.
  • Screenshot everything — hotel confirmations, Universal tickets, the AMC parking barcode, Bowl tickets. Cell service at the Bowl gets rough when 17,000 people arrive at once.
  • Nothing visible in either car, ever. Especially the Venice lot Tuesday.
  • A layer each for Thursday night. Outdoor venue, canyon, after dark.
  • Comfortable shoes for Wednesday. Universal is 6–8 miles of walking with a serious hill between the Upper and Lower Lots.
