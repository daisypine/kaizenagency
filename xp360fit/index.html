The big idea

What we found

Five things holding the boxing pages back

Ordered by how much each one is costing you. The first two are the big ones.

Finding 01 · High impact

The Boxing Academy lives on someone else's address

The menu link xp360fit.com/boxing-academy sends people to xp360-boxing-academy.lovable.app using a 302 (temporary) redirect. That tells Google "the real page is somewhere else, for now," so it never fully credits either one.

  • The Lovable site has no robots.txt (it returns a 404) and no sitemap.
  • None of the Lovable pages declare a canonical URL pointing back to xp360fit.com.
  • The Squarespace sitemap doesn't list the Boxing Academy or the Fundamentals pages at all.
Why it mattersThese are your most detailed, most boxing-focused pages, and they're working for a free subdomain instead of your domain.
The fixPut the Academy on your own domain. See Owning it below for three ways to do that, including one that keeps the Lovable/AI workflow.
Finding 02 · High impact

Two of the three homepage programs lead to a dead end

The "Our Programs" section on the homepage has three cards. Only Strength & Conditioning works. The two boxing cards are broken:

  • Boxing Conditioning → /our-programssection → page not found
  • 1 on 1 Boxing Coaching → /1-on-1-boxing → page not found
Why it mattersSomeone interested in boxing clicks once and hits an error. Google sees homepage links pointing at missing pages, which drags down trust in the whole site.
The fixPoint both cards at live boxing pages (on your domain) today. In Squarespace that's a two-minute link edit, and add 301 redirects for the old URLs under Settings → URL Mappings.
Finding 03 · Medium impact

The homepage barely mentions boxing

Google reads the title and main headline first. Right now they're about strength, not boxing:

  • Page title: HOME | Xp360 Strength & Conditioning Gym | Sunnyvale, CA. The "HOME |" wastes the most valuable words.
  • Main headline: "Confidence Starts with Strength." Boxing doesn't appear until the program cards.
  • The meta description is printed twice back-to-back, which looks like a Squarespace settings duplicate.
The fixSomething like Strength & Boxing Gym in Sunnyvale, CA | XP360 for the title, a subheadline that names beginner boxing, and one clean meta description.
Finding 04 · Medium impact

Old and duplicate pages are still out there

  • The sitemap doesn't list the homepage (xp360fit.com/) at all. In its place, with top priority, is /new-page, which shows the exact same content. That usually means a new homepage was built as "new-page" and set as the homepage without changing its URL. Google now sees two addresses for one page and is being told the wrong one matters.
  • The whole sitemap is only 12 pages, and none of them is a working boxing page.
  • /boxing-workshop still advertises an April 26, 2025 workshop, and its title repeats the brand three times.
  • /covid-19 is still in the sitemap.
  • On Lovable, Fundamentals I says the Summer 2026 session is sold out, and Fundamentals II lists an August 11, 2026 start date.
Why it mattersOutdated dates make a busy gym look inactive, and duplicates confuse Google about which page to rank.
The fixMake sure the homepage lives only at xp360fit.com/ (rename the page's slug and 301 /new-page to the root), and retire /covid-19. Turn the workshop page into an evergreen "Beginner Boxing in Sunnyvale" page. Swap fixed dates for "Next session starts…" plus a waitlist.
Finding 05 · Quick wins

Small things a new visitor notices

  • The newsletter block at the bottom of the homepage shows an unfinished setup message to visitors ("This newsletter signup form needs a storage option").
  • Image file names use pipes and no extensions (like xp360|boxing+conditioning|...), so image search can't read them well.
  • Messy URLs like /faqs-1 and /our-programssection.
  • Most Squarespace sitemap pages haven't been updated since mid-2025.
The fixRemove or connect the newsletter block, rename key images (beginner-boxing-class-sunnyvale.jpg), and clean up slugs with redirects.

Owning your stuff

Keep building with AI, keep it on your domain

You already own the domain and the Squarespace site. The gap is that new AI-built pages get published somewhere else. There are three clean ways to close it.

Fastest · this week

A. Put Lovable on a subdomain

Connect boxing.xp360fit.com to the Lovable project. Lovable supports this on paid plans, and the DNS record goes in Squarespace.

  • Keeps your Lovable workflow
  • Pages carry your name, not lovable.app
  • Subdomains still split some credit from the main site
Best for ranking · 2–3 weeks

B. Rebuild the boxing pages in Squarespace

Use AI to write and design the pages, then build them as real Squarespace pages at xp360fit.com/boxing, /boxing/fundamentals-1 and so on.

  • All credit goes to xp360fit.com
  • One site, one sitemap, one login
  • Fixes the broken homepage links at the same time
Long game

C. Move the whole site to an owned build

Rebuild xp360fit.com on a fast, fully owned site so AI-made pages can be added straight to your domain anytime.

  • Total control, no platform limits
  • Best for speed and future pages
  • Bigger project, so it makes sense once A or B is done

Questions

Good to know

Do we have to stop using Lovable?

No. AI tools are a great way to draft pages fast. The only change is where the finished page gets published: on xp360fit.com (or a subdomain of it), not on a lovable.app address.

Will moving the pages hurt anything that's working now?

Not if it's done with proper 301 redirects. Anyone with an old link still lands on the right page, and Google transfers credit to the new address.

How fast will rankings change?

The Week 1 fixes are picked up within days to a few weeks once Google recrawls. New boxing pages typically take 1–3 months to settle into local results. Your strong reviews help speed that up.

Do we keep owning everything?

Yes. Domain, Squarespace account, Google Business Profile and Search Console stay in your name. We work as a collaborator on your accounts.
