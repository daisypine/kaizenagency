The Same Team, Every Single Visit

Most cleaning companies send whoever is available. You get a different person each visit, and you explain your home all over again every time.

We don't work that way. L&A is owned by Lidia and Aurelio, who have built and trained a team that's been cleaning homes on the Peninsula for more than ten years. We assign the same cleaners to your home, so by the third visit nobody needs to be told which floors need the gentle products or where the vacuum lives.

We're licensed and bonded, and we bring our own supplies. If anyone in your home is sensitive to fragrance or you'd rather we skip the harsh chemicals, just ask - we keep all-natural products on hand at no extra charge.

Common Questions

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

No. Most of our regular clients give us a key or a door code and come home to a clean house. Whatever you're comfortable with works for us.

Will I get the same cleaners each time?

Yes, that's how we schedule it. We assign a consistent team to your home so you're not re-explaining your preferences every visit.

Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?

Yes, everything. If you'd prefer we use a specific product on a particular surface, leave it out and let us know.

Do you clean Airbnbs and short-term rentals?

Yes. We handle regular turnovers between guests on whatever schedule your bookings run, plus deeper periodic cleans to keep the property in shape.
