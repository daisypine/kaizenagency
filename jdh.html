Our story
It started with a bike and a backpack of tools. Jose rode job to job, taking care of one yard at a time — and never stopped showing up.

That solo hustle grew into a full Peninsula crew, but the approach hasn't changed: work hard, do it right, and treat every property like it's our own. Today we handle everything from weekly lawn care to irrigation, hardscaping, and complete makeovers — for homeowners and businesses across Redwood City and the Peninsula.
