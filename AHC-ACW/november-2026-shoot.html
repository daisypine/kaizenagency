Shoot day: Thursday, Nov 5

1 hour with Eli, Dr. Paris and Dr. Kyndra. Mostly exercise and education, with 3 quick trends to break it up: 9 educational pieces, 3 trends and a B-roll montage. That's enough for 2–3 weeks of posts.

How it works: watch the reference, then film our version. Vertical, handheld, words on screen, not memorized. Filming is grouped by room so nobody waits around.

Who picks the exercises: whoever is on camera (Eli, Dr. Paris or Dr. Kyndra). The references show the format, not the exact moves. Dr. Kyndra signs off on anything clinical before it posts.

Short on time? Must-films are marked ★: #1, #4, #5, #7, #9 and #10.

2. Bicep heart trend Trend Eli + Dr. Paris

Reference: @s_chodenn "the bicep in question" (29.6K likes, sent by Dr. Kyndra)

3. Choose your character: Adapt edition Trend Eli + Dr. Paris

Reference: @balancedbodyjm "Choose your character, massage therapist edition"

  • Shot: each does a video-game "character select" pose against the same wall.
  • Text: Choose your character + 3 stats each: Role (chiropractor / trainer) · Specialty · Says this every session

4. Tight hips: 4-minute mobility routine ★ Exercise Eli

Reference: @coachgreen.pt "TIGHT HIPS, 4 minute hip mobility routine"

  • Format: 4 moves, one clip each, same angle, with the move name and time on screen ("Move 1 · 60 sec").
  • Hook: Tight hips? Do this every morning for a week.
  • Caption idea: save this + "want a plan built for your body? Book with Eli, link in bio."
  • Links to: the hip pain and personal training pages

5. Low back pain: 3 moves ★ Exercise Dr. Paris

References: @coachgreen.pt "Low back pain mobility" · @coachgreen.pt "hip mobility & core stability"

  • Format: 3 gentle moves on a mat, side angle so the spine is visible. One clip each, with the name on screen.
  • Hook: Low back stiff when you get out of bed? Try these 3.
  • End text: Still stiff after 2 weeks? That's what we're here for.
  • Links to: the back pain page

6. Bulletproof ankles Exercise Eli

References: @move_withagnes "Save this for bulletproof ankles" · @backpaintip "your upper back and ankles might be limiting your movement"

  • Format: 3 ankle moves, close-up on the feet, then a wide shot.
  • Hook: Rolled your ankle once and it's never felt the same? Save this.
  • Fits: athletes and runners, Adapt's main patients. Links to sports injury.

7. Desk posture reset ★ Exercise Dr. Paris

Reference: @move_withagnes "To fix your posture, save this"

  • Links to: neck pain and headaches pages

8. Shoulder strength for loose joints Exercise Dr. Paris

Reference: @thatchirowithtattoos "Hypermobile girlies, let's strengthen your shoulders"

  • Moves: Dr. Paris picks the 5 exercises on the day.

10. Neck pain: one rehab exercise ★ Education Dr. Kyndra

  • Links to: the neck pain page

11. "Does shockwave hurt?" Honest answer Education Dr. Kyndra

11b. "Is that cracking sound bad?" Adjustment myth Education Dr. Paris

Reference: none needed. Classic chiro myth-buster format: question on screen, quick answer, real footage.

  • Shot: Dr. Paris adjusting Eli on the table (film it in the treatment room while Dr. Kyndra resets). Then Dr. Paris to camera for 10 seconds.
  • Text: Is that cracking sound your bones? → Nope. It's gas releasing from the joint. → The sound isn't the goal. Better movement is.
  • Caption idea: what actually happens at a first visit, plus "book with Dr. Paris, link in bio."
  • Check: Dr. Paris words the explanation himself, so it's accurate and in his voice.
  • Links to: the chiropractic care page

12. "It's not just a massage" B-roll montage Montage Everyone

Reference: @eliteptnj "Physical therapy isn't just massages: real movement, real exercise, real results"

  • Shots (2–3 sec each): Eli coaching a set · Dr. Paris adjusting · hands-on cues · shockwave · laser · HBOT chamber · adjusting table · Nori if she's in.
  • Text: Chiropractic isn't just cracking backs. → It's real movement. Real strength. Real results.
  • Bonus: extra clips fill future stories.
