Every idea: shot, text and reference

1. Bicep heart trend Eli + Dr. Paris

Reference: @s_chodenn "the bicep in question" (29.6K likes), sent by Dr. Kyndra. More examples: @justnostalgia.jnt #BicepHeartTrend roundup

  • Shot: Eli and Dr. Paris face each other, flex, and touch biceps so the two arms make a heart. Film in the gym area with a slow push-in on the heart.
  • Text: POV: your trainers are also your biggest fans 💚
  • Audio: the trending sound on the reference
  • Tip: film first to warm everyone up. No script needed.

2. "Friendly reminder" hip reel Dr. Kyndra

Reference: @ericadekle.physicaltherapy ("friendly reminder that if your hips cannot internally rotate, your lower back and SI joint are probably hurting"). Same idea as the 90/90 reel in the November plan.

  • Shot: Dr. Kyndra on a mat in the rehab area doing slow 90/90 hip switches, 5 reps, then a close-up of the hips turning. She looks up and smiles at the end.
  • Text 1: friendly reminder that if your hips can't rotate, your low back is probably picking up the slack
  • Text 2: xoxo, your Campbell chiropractor
  • Text 3: try 5 slow reps each side · pinching or sharp pain? stop and get it checked
  • Check: Dr. Kyndra approves the wording before posting.

3. Chiro stats like Strava Dr. Kyndra

Reference: @dr.kristiblack "Sharing my Chiro stats like Strava posts". Dr. Kyndra sent this one; it's in the trendy content bucket.

  • Shot: Dr. Kyndra walking into the clinic, down the hallway and through the treatment room. One continuous walk, phone held steady.
  • Text (in edit, styled like a workout summary): Adjustments: 14 · Coffees: 2 · "I'll do my stretches at home": 6 · Nori pets: 9
  • Tip: film the walk now, write the stats later. Ask Dr. Kyndra for her real numbers from the day.

4. Walk-to-work selfie Dr. Kyndra

5. Choose your trainer Eli + Dr. Paris

Reference: @balancedbodyjm "CHOOSE YOUR CHARACTER, massage therapist edition"

  • Shot: each trainer does a quick video-game "character select" pose, one at a time, against the same wall.
  • Text: Choose your trainer, then 3 quick stats per person: Specialty · Favorite exercise · Says this every session. Ask them for their answers on the day.
  • Why: sells personal training without sounding like an ad.

6. "You spent years sitting" exercise demo Eli or Dr. Paris

Reference: @the_woman_reclaimed "You spent years carrying the weight" (58.1K likes)

  • Shot: 3 floor exercises for desk workers, one clip each, on a mat. The trainer picks the moves.

  • Check: Dr. Kyndra glances at the exercise picks before posting.
