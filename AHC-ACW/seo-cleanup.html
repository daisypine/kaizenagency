Better Search Replace — four passes

Before running:
  • Duplicator backup, fresh. Not the nightly one.
  • SiteGround staging first, then push.
  • Tick "Run as dry run" and check the match count before committing.
  • Never raw SQL. Elementor stores content serialised in wp_postmeta — a plain SQL replace breaks the length prefixes and blanks every page. BSR handles serialisation.

Pass 1 — brand name

Search:  Adapt Health Clinic
Replace: Adapt Chiropractic & Wellness

Footer, Dr. Grossman's "Founder of Adapt Health Clinic", Dr. Kyndra's "Chiropractor at Adapt Health Clinic", stray mentions in body copy and meta.

Pass 2 — booking subdomain

Search:  adapthealthclinic.janeapp.com
Replace: adaptchiropracticwellness.janeapp.com

Every Book Online button site-wide.

Pass 3 — typo

Search:  Nathalia Ferreyr     ← trailing space
Replace: Nathalia Ferreyra    ← trailing space
Trailing spaces matter. "Ferreyr" is a substring of "Ferreyra". Without the space this turns every correct "Ferreyra" into "Ferreyraa".

Pass 4 — old domain

Search:  https://adapthealthclinic.com
Replace: https://adaptchiropracticwellness.com
Anchor on https://. Searching the bare adapthealthclinic.com rewrites the live email addresses too — info@, drmike@, michaeld@ are all still receiving mail.
After all four: purge SiteGround cache, then Elementor → Tools → Regenerate CSS & Data. Spot-check the footer, both team pages, and a booking button on mobile.
