What's Walking In Our Door Lately

Elbow pain when you grip
Usually tennis elbow, and rest alone rarely fixes it.

TRY THIS AT HOME

  • Lift palm-up, like carrying a tray, while it heals.
  • Loosen your grip on the mouse, tools or racket. A death grip loads the tendon all day.
  • A forearm strap can take the edge off during work or play.
Learn how we treat tennis elbow →
Heel pain on your first steps
Worst in the morning? Usually plantar fasciitis.

TRY THIS AT HOME

  • Before you stand up in the morning, point and flex your foot 10 to 15 times.
  • Skip going barefoot on hard floors for now. What you stand on all day matters.
  • Slow calf raises a few times a week build the strength your foot needs.
Learn how we treat heel pain →
Achilles sore after a run
Stiff in the morning, flares up when you train.

TRY THIS AT HOME

  • Keep calf stretches gentle. Skip hanging your heel off a step.
  • Strength beats stretching here: slow heel raises usually help more.
  • Mild pain on a run that's gone by the next morning is usually OK. Felt a pop? Get checked the same day.
