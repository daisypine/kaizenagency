Email strategy

Current emails inform. They don't ask for anything, so they don't produce bookings. One send per month, rotating through four jobs:

1
Educate → book

Lead with a real problem, explain it, end with a booking link. Pulls from that month's blog content.

2
Offer → act now

Dated, specific reason to book this month. The $225 first visit, a seasonal package, an intro session.

3
Referral & proof

Patient story, then an explicit ask. 57% of patients already come this way and the list has never been asked.

4
Reactivate

90+ days out. Short, low pressure. The SMS version already produced a booking.

Offer ladder: $225 first visit → referral incentive → Q4 gift card → HBOT or shockwave intro → partnership program.

Blocked on three client decisions: what he'll package or discount, gift card denominations and terms, referral incentive.
