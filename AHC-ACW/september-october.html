Send + reply from your adaptcw.com email

Please make sure your emails send and reply from your adaptcw.com address. It has to be done from inside your Gmail, and it takes about 2 minutes.

Open my Gmail settings
  1. In Gmail, click the gear icon (top right) > See all settings. The button above skips this step.
  2. Go to the Accounts tab > Send mail as > Add another email address.
  3. Enter drmike@adaptcw.com, leave Treat as an alias checked, and click Next step. If it asks for a code, it'll show up in your inbox.
  4. Back on that page, click make default next to drmike@adaptcw.com.
  5. Under When replying to a message, choose Always reply from default address.
Same steps on the info@ inbox with info@adaptcw.com. To check it worked, send yourself a test email and make sure it says it's from @adaptcw.com. Or we can hop on a quick call and I'll walk you through it. Google's own guide is here.
