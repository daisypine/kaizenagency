What the crawl turned up

Five things nobody had flagged. Two of them change the September plan.

1. Booking links on the live site still point at the old domain.
Every "Book Online" button resolves to adapthealthclinic.janeapp.com. Working only because Jane is redirecting. If that lapses, bookings fail silently.
2. Duplicate prenatal pages.
/prenatal-chiropractor/ and /prenatal-chiropractor-campbell/ are both live, both targeting the same term, splitting their own ranking. Pick one, 301 the other into it.
3. Two team page role lines still say "Adapt Health Clinic."
Dr. Grossman's reads "Founder of Adapt Health Clinic." Dr. Kyndra's reads "Chiropractor at Adapt Health Clinic" and her body copy mixes both names.
4. The Techniques page is still live at /techniques/, despite Dr. Mike explicitly asking for it to be deleted — "you're like using tools or tape or something. So I'd probably just delete that page altogether."
5. /partnerships/ already exists.
It was on the September list as a page to build. It's live. Audit whether it has real content — that changes September from a build to a rewrite.
Also worth knowing: condition URLs use /conditions/[name]/, not the -campbell-ca pattern in the old migration sheet. All new pages follow what shipped. And /service-areas/san-jose/ exists — a pattern we could extend to other cities, carefully.
