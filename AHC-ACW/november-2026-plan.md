## How to make Adapt better

1. **Own the athlete space.** Build one injury page a month for the next 3 months (rotator cuff, hamstring, IT band next). Then add pages by sport: runners, golfers, lifters, cyclists.
2. **Build a sports rehab service page.** It shows what's different: adjustments, soft tissue work, then strength training with Eli or Dr. Paris until the patient is back to their sport. No one nearby has this.
3. **Target San Jose too.** Icon does it from the same street in Campbell. Add San Jose wording to the homepage and service pages, without changing URLs.
4. **Make shockwave the hero.** Add FAQs, real photos, and which conditions it helps. Good Life is the one to beat.
5. **Lower the barrier to the first visit.** Every competitor has a $50–$59 intro or a free consult. Even a free 15-minute call or a shockwave intro session would help.
6. **Use video.** Positive Motion's patients say they found them on YouTube. The monthly content shoot can feed Reels, YouTube Shorts and the pages.
7. **Keep the review lead.** Keep replying personally to every Google review, and ask every patient for one (never only the happy ones, and no incentives).
