The Falcon Roof Health Report

A free, written, photographed inspection of the whole roof, with an honest answer on how many years it has left, what needs doing now, what can wait, and what each would cost. The homeowner keeps it whether they hire Falcon or not.

Why we're doing it

Everyone offers a "free estimate". This gives the homeowner something they can actually hold, so Falcon gets in the door before the rain and the report sells the work on its own. It's the free offer on the mailer, every email and the new Roof Health Report page on the site.

What we check on site

The inspector's checklist. Drone shots are a bonus on 2-story or steep roofs.

Roof coveringWide + close-upsMaterial, age, shingle wear and granule loss, cracked, missing or lifted shingles or tiles, moss, soft spots
FlashingPhotoChimney, skylights, walls, valleys: rust, gaps, failed sealant
PenetrationsPhotoVent pipe boots, exhaust vents, solar mounts. Cracked boots are the #1 small leak.

InsideIf anyCeiling stains, especially top floor and closets. Ask the homeowner.

Property managers + HOAs

Same report, one per building, plus a one-page summary across all buildings ranked by priority, so the manager can take it straight to an owner or a board.

The rules

  • No dollar value on the report and no discounts or referral rewards (California B&P §7157).
  • Be honest. If the roof is fine, say so. That's what earns the next job and the review.
  • Warranty wording is 10-year workmanship. The September mailer copy still says "lifetime", so it has to change before it prints.
  • Photos from reports can be used in marketing only with the homeowner's OK, by neighborhood, never with the address.
