How a lead moves through Falcon

We looked at every lead in Housecall Pro since June 19. The leads are coming in, but almost none of them get moved forward inside the system. That means nobody can see what was called back, and the work that brings leads in can't be measured.

161leads in Housecall Pro since Jun 19
157still in "New Lead", unassigned
4moved to an estimate from the lead
28old website form leads (Mar–Sep), 17 never entered in HCP

Where leads come from: 144 Yelp (109 are gutter cleaning), 11 website lead form, 6 entered by hand.

Estimates: 34 sent and waiting on an answer, 12 declined. These are the fastest wins: one follow-up call each.

The simple flow we're asking the team to follow

  1. New lead lands (website form, Yelp, phone) → it goes into Housecall Pro the same day. Phone calls too.
  2. Assign it to one person and call back within 1 business day.
  3. Move the stage: Contacted → Estimate scheduled → Estimate sent. Don't create the estimate outside the lead.
  4. Close it out: mark it Won (job booked) or Lost with a reason. Never leave it sitting in New Lead.
  5. Follow up on every sent estimate at 3 days and 10 days.

The full call-back list (who, what they asked for, call order and a short script) is in the team's Leads to Call Back sheet.
