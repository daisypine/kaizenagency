The 5 rules for every new lead

  1. Into Housecall Pro same day. Website, Yelp, phone. All of it.
  2. One owner. Assign it, call back within 1 business day.
  3. Move the stage. Contacted → Estimate scheduled → Estimate sent.
  4. Close it. Won or Lost with a reason. Nothing sits in "New Lead".
  5. Follow up every estimate at day 3 and day 10.
