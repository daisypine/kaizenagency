Q&AQuestions I get a lot

What I'd recommend depends on where you're at.

Do I need a whole new website?

Not always. Sometimes it just needs a cleanup. Sometimes it makes more sense to start fresh. I'll tell you which.

I don't have the login to my site or domain.

Super common. Step one is getting you ownership back, so you can actually make changes.

Should I add a chat widget?

Sometimes yes, sometimes no. It depends on your industry and what your data says.

What should my website actually do?

Whatever the main thing in your business is: calls, bookings, quotes or tickets. We build around that.

I already see numbers in other tools.

Those estimate. Google Analytics and Search Console on your own site are your true data.

I'm just getting started.

Start simple and clean, and set it up so it can grow with you.

A question I getAbout AI

"Can't I just build my website with AI?" You can.

AI is great at a first draft. It doesn't know:

  1. What your business needs next
  2. What makes your customers trust you
  3. What your data is telling you
  4. If it all works across your site, Google, email and print
100+checks behind every site I launch
  • Google Analytics + Search Console, so you see which pages actually get traffic
  • Compliance for your industry, so you don't get sued
  • Security, so you're not constantly getting hacked
  • Every form tested and landing where it should
  • Old pages redirected so you keep your rankings
