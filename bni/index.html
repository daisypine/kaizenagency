A question I getAbout AI

"Can't I just build my website with AI?" You can.

AI is great at a first draft. It doesn't know:

  1. What your business needs next
  2. What makes your customers trust you
  3. Which pages, offers and follow-up should exist
  4. If it all works across your site, Google, email and print
  5. What to improve next month
100+checks before a site goes live
  • Your phone number is right on every page and button
  • Every form actually reaches your inbox
  • Old pages redirect so you keep your Google rankings
  • California privacy and cookie rules are covered
  • Tap-to-call works on a real phone
