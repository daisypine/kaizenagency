Voice and tone

Direct, warm, and confident about price

Sound like a family business that runs like a real company. Lead with the customer's problem, name the city, the building and the finish. "We're not the cheapest. Here's what that buys."

Always written Sunwest Painting, one word with a capital S. Not "Sun West."

Do

  • Be specific: Los Gatos Victorian, HOA exterior, after-hours office repaint
  • Show the prep, the checklist and the walkthrough
  • Use short hyphens, not long dashes
  • Use real photos of real Sunwest jobs

Don't

  • "Quality workmanship, satisfaction guaranteed"
  • Discounts, countdowns or fake urgency
  • Promise timelines to win a bid
  • Start every sentence with "we"

Who we talk to

Homeowners

  • Custom home owners who value craft over the lowest price
  • Higher-end areas: Los Gatos, Palo Alto, Los Altos, Saratoga
  • Pre-sale and vacant homes that need a fast, clean interior
  • People burned by a cheaper painter who want it done right

Commercial

  • Property managers with multi-unit, repeat work
  • Facilities managers with scheduled maintenance
  • General contractors who need a reliable painting sub
  • HOA boards on exterior refresh cycles

Core area: San Jose, Santa Clara, Sunnyvale, Campbell, Los Gatos, Saratoga. Higher ticket: Palo Alto, Los Altos, Mountain View and up the Peninsula.

13

What we promote

Promote

  • Residential interior and exterior painting
  • Commercial painting, all verticals
  • After-hours painting for occupied buildings
  • Historic home restoration in Palo Alto and Los Gatos
  • Epoxy floors, residential and warehouse
  • Color consultation

Don't build pages for

  • Cabinet painting on its own
  • Popcorn ceiling or wallpaper removal
  • Pressure washing
  • Minor drywall repair on its own
  • Never: full drywall installs or major texture work

These are offered but low margin or high risk, so they stay off ads, pages and campaigns.

The funnel

Get found

Show up where neighbors and managers look

  • Google Business Profile, weekly posts
  • Service, city and industry pages
  • Door hangers and yard signs at every job
  • EDDM mail to high-ticket routes

Build trust

Prove the craft before the call

  • Project pages with real photos
  • Before and after pairs
  • Google reviews and owner replies
  • License, insurance and EPA RRP on every page

Ask for the job

One clear next step per page

  • Request a quote form
  • Commercial bid request form
  • Call 408-623-1603
  • Thank-you page with what happens next

Come back

Turn every job into the next one

  • Estimate follow-up by email and text
  • Review request after the walkthrough
  • Referral ask and monthly newsletter
  • Repaint reminder years later

Every piece of print gets a QR code and every form lands on the thank-you page, so each stage can be measured.
