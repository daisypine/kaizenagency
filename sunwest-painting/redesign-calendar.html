How a rebuild works

  1. Duplicate the live page as a draft. Never edit the live page directly.
  2. Build it on the template, using the copy from that page's tab in the copy doc. Keep every fact, photo, FAQ and link that's on the page now.
  3. Keep the same URL, SEO title and meta description.
  4. Send Daisy the draft link. Once she OKs it, move the new layout onto the live page, purge the SiteGround cache and check it logged out on a phone.
  5. Update the Status column in the sitemap.

Arrows: circled diagonal arrows on hero buttons, industry tiles, portfolio cards and "View more projects". Plain horizontal arrows on service and city link lists. No arrows in the quote form section. No emojis. Short hyphens only.
