How a rebuild works

  1. Duplicate the live page as a draft. Never edit the live page directly.
  2. Insert the saved template for that layout. Paste the copy from the page's tab in the copy doc.
  3. Keep the same URL, SEO title and meta, photos, FAQs and links.
  4. Send Daisy the draft link. Once she OKs it, move the new layout onto the live page, purge the SiteGround cache and check it logged out on a phone.
  5. Mark it done in the sitemap.

Arrows: circled diagonal arrows on hero buttons, industry tiles, portfolio cards and "View more projects". Plain horizontal arrows on service and city link lists. No arrows in the quote form section. No emojis. Short hyphens only. No street names or homeowner names.

Launch check

Every rebuilt page, before we call it done:

  • ☐ Nothing missing compared with its copy doc tab: sections, FAQs, photos, links
  • ☐ One H1. Section titles are H2 or H3
  • ☐ SEO title ends in "| Sunwest Painting", under 60 characters. Meta description under 155
  • ☐ Same URL as before. No broken links or 404s. Any changed URL has a 301 redirect
  • ☐ Every image has alt text. No stock-photo placeholders like "Rectangle 40236"
  • ☐ Buttons go to the right place. Forms submit and land on the thank-you page
  • ☐ Header and footer are the new ones. Looks right on a phone, logged out
  • ☐ No [CONFIRM] left in, no long dashes, no street or homeowner names
  • ☐ SiteGround cache purged. Sitemap resubmitted in Search Console when everything is live
