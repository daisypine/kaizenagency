Sunwest Painting is a family-owned commercial and residential painting contractor in San Jose. They've been operating since 2001 and run multiple crews out of one location. Denise's parents are still involved — dad runs project management, mom handles the books — and both are moving toward retirement in the next two to three years. A stated goal of this engagement is helping the business run without depending on family labor.

What they're actually good at

  • Custom, detail-oriented finish work. They take their time. Trim, doors, custom homes, historic restorations. Work that holds up to close inspection.
  • Communication. Prep checklists, updates during the job, a final walkthrough and sign-off. Most competitors are owner-operators who just show up and paint.
  • Both sides of the business. High-end custom residential and large commercial or institutional projects, from one shop.

Why they lose bids — important for copy

Almost always price. Competitors are currently undercutting by $3,000–$5,000 on a typical home. Sunwest does not compete on price and our copy should never imply they do. The job is to make the difference feel like a reason to choose them — craft, communication, and credentials — not a hurdle.

What We're Building For Them

Engagement started April 2026. The new website went live in July on a rebuilt Elementor stack. We're now publishing out a ~100-page site architecture — services, industries, city pages, and combined service-plus-city pages — built around keyword research rather than guesses.

Alongside the site: brand collateral (business cards, flyer, trifold, folder, door hangers), Google Business Profile optimization, a review program, and a growth roadmap covering direct mail, email, SMS, and CRM.

Services — What to Promote, What Not To

This matters more than it looks. Some services are offered but deliberately not promoted because they're low margin or high risk. Don't build pages or write campaigns around them.

✓ Promote heavily
  • Residential interior & exterior painting
  • Commercial painting — all verticals
  • After-hours / PM painting (differential rate, low competition)
  • Historic home restoration (Palo Alto, Los Gatos)
  • Epoxy floors — residential & warehouse
  • Color consultation
✕ Don't promote standalone
  • Cabinet painting — slow, low margin, error-prone
  • Popcorn ceiling removal
  • Wallpaper removal
  • Pressure washing
  • Minor drywall repair
  • Never: full drywall installation or major texture work

Tone

✓ Do
  • Direct and specific — name the city, the building, the finish
  • Confident about price: "We're not the cheapest. Here's what that buys."
  • Lead with the customer's problem, not company history
  • Warm but professional — a family business that runs like a real company
✕ Don't
  • "Quality workmanship, satisfaction guaranteed"
  • Discount language, urgency gimmicks, fake countdowns
  • Overpromise timelines to win a bid
  • Start every sentence with "we"

Written as Sunwest Painting — one word, capital S. Not "Sun West."

What we need more of

  • Before / after pairs — same angle, same light. Nothing converts like it.
  • Detail shots — crisp cut lines, trim, clean edges. The craft argument, made visually.
  • Crew at work — branded gear, clean organized site, drop cloths down.
  • Commercial scale — lifts, large facades, after-hours interiors with lights on.

Natural color, no heavy filters, straight verticals. Darken the image behind text rather than outlining the type.
